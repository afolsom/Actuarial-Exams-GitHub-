## Answer Key & Solutions — Practice Exam 2

1. **(B) 0.25**  
   $P(L \cup P) = 1 - 0.50 = 0.50$.  
   $P(L \cap P^c) = P(L \cup P) - P(P) = 0.50 - 0.25 = \mathbf{0.25}$.

2. **(B) 0.48**  
   By Inclusion-Exclusion: $P(A \cup H \cup L) = (0.30 + 0.20 + 0.15) - (0.10 + 0.05 + 0.04) + 0.02 = 0.65 - 0.19 + 0.02 = \mathbf{0.48}$.

3. **(D) 0.1824**  
   Total outcomes $\binom{18}{5} = 8568$.  
   Favorable ($X \ge 4$): $\binom{9}{4}\binom{9}{1} + \binom{9}{5}\binom{9}{0} = 126(9) + 126(1) = 1134 + 126 = 1560$.  
   $P = \frac{1560}{8568} = \mathbf{0.1824}$.

4. **(B) 0.20**  
   By Bonferroni's Inequality: $P(A \cap B) \ge P(A) + P(B) - 1 = 0.70 + 0.50 - 1.00 = \mathbf{0.20}$.

5. **(B) 0.667**  
   $P(C \mid +) = \frac{P(+\mid C)P(C)}{P(+\mid C)P(C) + P(+\mid C^c)P(C^c)} = \frac{(0.90)(0.10)}{(0.90)(0.10) + (0.05)(0.90)} = \frac{0.090}{0.090 + 0.045} = \frac{0.090}{0.135} = \mathbf{0.667}$.

6. **(A) 0.720**  
   $P(\text{At least one}) = 1 - P(A^c)P(B^c)P(C^c) = 1 - (0.80)(0.70)(0.50) = 1 - 0.280 = \mathbf{0.720}$.

7. **(C) 0.50**  
   $\binom{10}{3} = 120$. Favorable: $\binom{4}{1}\binom{6}{2} = 4 \times 15 = 60$.  
   $P = \frac{60}{120} = \mathbf{0.50}$.

8. **(A) 14,400**  
   1st char: 9 ways. 2nd & 3rd chars: $5 \times 4 = 20$ ways. 4th & 5th chars: $10 \times 10 = 100$ ways.  
   Total $= 9 \times 20 \times 100 = \mathbf{14,400}$.

9. **(C) 0.1954**  
   For Poisson, $Var(N) = E[N] = \lambda$.  
   $\lambda + \lambda = 8 \implies 2\lambda = 8 \implies \lambda = 4$.  
   $P(N = 2) = \frac{e^{-4} 4^2}{2!} = \frac{16 e^{-4}}{2} = 8 e^{-4} \approx \mathbf{0.1954}$.

10. **(E) 8.33**
