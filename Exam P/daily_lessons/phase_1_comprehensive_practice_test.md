## Detailed Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
- **Correct Answer:** **A**
- **Derivation:**
  1. "Liability coverage ($L$) and..." requires an intersection with $L$: $L \cap (\dots)$.
  2. "at least one of Collision ($C$) or Comprehensive ($K$)" translates to $(C \cup K)$.
  3. "but does not have both Collision and Comprehensive" means excluding the joint intersection $(C \cap K)$, represented by $(C \cap K)^c$.
  4. Combining all components yields $L \cap (C \cup K) \cap (C \cap K)^c$.

### Solution 2
- **Correct Answer:** **C**
- **Derivation:**
  1. Apply De Morgan's Law to $(E \cup F^c)^c$:
     $$(E \cup F^c)^c = E^c \cap (F^c)^c = E^c \cap F$$
  2. Now substitute back into the expression:
     $$(E^c \cap F) \cup (E \cap F)$$
  3. Apply the Distributive Law in reverse (factoring out $\cap F$):
     $$(E^c \cup E) \cap F$$
  4. Since $E^c \cup E = S$ (the total sample space):
     $$S \cap F = F \cap E^c = E^c \cap F$$

### Solution 7
- **Correct Answer:** **B**

     $$6! = 720 \text{ ways}$$
  2. Placing 6 items creates $6 + 1 = 7$ available gaps.
