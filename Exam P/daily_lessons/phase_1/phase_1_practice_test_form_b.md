## Worked Solutions

### **1. Answer: (D)**
Apply the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion for three sets:
$$|A \cup H \cup U| = |A| + |H| + |U| - |A \cap H| - |A \cap U| - |H \cap U| + |A \cap H \cap U|$$
$$|A \cup H \cup U| = 310 + 240 + 120 - 150 - 80 - 65 + 40 = 415$$
The number of policyholders with none of the three coverages is:
$$500 - 415 = 85$$

### **2. Answer: (A)**
Use the Fundamental Counting Principle:
* 1st letter: 26 choices
* 2nd letter (distinct): 25 choices
* 1st digit (cannot be 0): 9 choices
* 2nd digit: 10 choices
* 3rd digit: 10 choices
$$\text{Total} = 26 \times 25 \times 9 \times 10 \times 10 = 585,000$$

### **3. Answer: (C)**
By De Morgan's Law, $P(A^c \cap B^c) = P((A \cup B)^c) = 1 - P(A \cup B) = 0.15$, so $P(A \cup B) = 0.85$.
Using the Addition Rule:
$$P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B) \implies 0.85 = 0.45 + 0.60 - P(A \cap B) \implies P(A \cap B) = 0.20$$
Now apply the definition of conditional probability:
$$P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)} = \frac{0.20}{0.60} = \frac{1}{3} \approx 0.333$$

### **4. Answer: (A)**

$$P(\text{all different colors}) = \frac{120}{455} = \frac{24}{91}$$

A fundamental theorem of independence states that if $A$ and $B$ are independent, then $(A, B^c)$, $(A^c, B)$, and $(A^c, B^c)$ are also independent pairs. (Mutually exclusive events with positive probabilities cannot be independent.)

### **30. Answer: (E)**
Let $E$ = exaggerated ($0.10$), $E^c$ = legitimate ($0.90$), $F$ = flagged by algorithm, and $M$ = confirmed by manual audit.
Since the algorithm and manual audit operate conditionally independently given the true claim status:
* Joint probability of $E \cap F \cap M$:
  $$P(E)P(F \mid E)P(M \mid E) = (0.10)(0.80)(0.90) = 0.0720$$
* Joint probability of $E^c \cap F \cap M$ (note: clears legitimate 95% $\implies$ confirms/fails to clear legitimate 5%):
  $$P(E^c)P(F \mid E^c)P(M \mid E^c) = (0.90)(0.10)(0.05) = 0.0045$$

Apply Bayes' Theorem:
$$P(E \mid F \cap M) = \frac{0.0720}{0.0720 + 0.0045} = \frac{0.0720}{0.0765} = \frac{720}{765} = \frac{16}{17} \approx 0.9412$$
